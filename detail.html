How to build

  1. Download the template

    Use the download button above to save the PDF.

  2. Print at 100% scale

    Turn off "fit to page" in your print settings. Use A4 cardstock, 160 to 220 gsm.

  3. Cut along the solid lines

    A craft knife and a metal ruler give the cleanest edges. Scissors work too.

  4. Fold along the dashed lines

    Score each fold lightly with a ruler and a blunt tool first, then fold.

  5. Glue the tabs

    Assemble the head first, then the body. Hold each tab for a few seconds until the glue grips.
