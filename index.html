From screen to shelf in four steps

All you need is a printer, paper, scissors and glue.

  1. Pick a template

    Choose a character and open its page. The difficulty is shown on every card.

  2. Print it

    Print at 100% scale on A4. Cardstock between 160 and 220 gsm holds its shape best.

  3. Cut and fold

    Cut along the solid lines and fold along the dashed ones. Scoring with a ruler gives crisp edges.

  4. Glue and display

    Match the tabs and glue piece by piece. Start with the head, then move to the body.
