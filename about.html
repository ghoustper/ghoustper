About GHOSTPER

Free anime papercraft templates, made for everyone who likes building things by hand.

GHOSTPER is a growing library of printable anime papercraft templates. Every template is free to download as a PDF, with a difficulty rating so you can choose a project that matches your skills.

What you get

  • Free PDF templates you can print at home
  • Clear cut and fold lines on every sheet
  • Easy, Medium and Hard difficulty levels
  • New characters added regularly

Want a specific character?

Tell us who you would like to build next. Open a request on GitHub and we will consider it for a future template.

Frequently asked questions

Are the templates really free?

Yes. Every template on GHOSTPER is free to download and print for personal use.

What paper should I use?

A4 cardstock between 160 and 220 gsm works best. Thinner paper is easier to fold but less sturdy.

What tools do I need?

A printer, scissors or a craft knife, a ruler, and white glue or double-sided tape.

Can I sell the finished papercrafts?

The templates are meant for personal, non-commercial use. The characters belong to their original creators, so please do not sell the templates or the finished models.
